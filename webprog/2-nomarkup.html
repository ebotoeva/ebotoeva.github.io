How to start watercolour Below you will find brief instructions on how to start with watercolours. Materials You will need the following supplies: - Watercolours: student-grade paint is a good place to start - Paper: proper watercolour paper, at least 300 gsm - Brushes: round with pointy tips - Clean water in a cup - Towels: cloth or paper towels Techniques To master the art of watercolour, one should master water control. Understanding how dry or wet the paper should be is the key. Wet-on-Wet technique This technique is great for creating soft blends of paper. Wet your paper first, then add paint. The colour will spread and mix on its own. To use for: skies, backgrounds, soft shadows. Tips: - a brush with a pointy tip lets you wet the paper with precision: the pigment will not spread to the dry bits of the paper - too much water may create puddles and colour may spread where it should not - painting over wet areas may give you undesired splotchy marks - not enough water means paint will not spread, so too much pigment may stay in one place Wet-on-Dry technique This technique is great for crisp lines and clean details. Apply paint and water to your brush and paint directly on dry paper. For more intense colour, get more paint and use a little less water...
